Hafez Music Ontology

A small instrument for reading Hafez through his musical vocabulary: couplets in which he names a minstrel, an instrument, a song, a mode or a dance.

The collection draws on his ghazals, qet'e, ruba'i, qasidas and the Saqi-nameh, and is gathered by twenty Persian words and their common forms:

مطرب، طرب، بربط، پرده، چنگ، رباب، بانگ، آهنگ، صوت، ساز، آواز، نی، رود، دف، رقص، سماع، دستان، کمانچه، نغمه، سرود

How the couplets were chosen

Several of these words lead double lives: رود is a river, a stringed instrument, or the verb “goes”; پرده is a veil, a fret, or a musical mode; نی is a reed flute or “not”; ساز is an instrument or the verb “make”. Each candidate couplet was read in context, and those where the word carries only a non-musical sense were left out. A handful of borderline cases (mostly plain “joy”, طرب, or an ordinary voice) are held back as well. Matched words are highlighted in each couplet.

The Persian text comes from Ganjoor, and every couplet links back to its page there. The English renderings are working translations prepared with Claude: literal rather than poetic, and open to correction.
