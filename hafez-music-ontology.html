Hafez Music Ontology, couplets of Hafez about music, with English translations

This project reads Hafez through his musical vocabulary. It gathers the couplets in which he names a minstrel, an instrument, a song, a mode or a dance, found through twenty Persian words and their common forms, and it leaves out the places where a word is used in a non-musical sense. You can draw a couplet at random, read it in English, or browse and search the whole collection. The Persian text comes from Ganjoor, the English is a working translation prepared with Claude, and the code is written by Claude Code and is available in my Github.
